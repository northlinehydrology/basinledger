# MODFLOW 6

The platform wheels of Basin Ledger carry MODFLOW 6's shared library (`libmf6.dylib`, `libmf6.so` or `libmf6.dll`),
unchanged, from the U.S. Geological Survey's release of MODFLOW 6, fetched with flopy's `get-modflow` when the wheel
is built.

MODFLOW 6 is U.S. Geological Survey software. Its source, release notes and license are at
https://github.com/MODFLOW-ORG/modflow6 (the license is in `LICENSE.md` there). Cite it as the USGS asks there.

Basin Ledger's own code is under the BSD 3-Clause license (`LICENSE`).
